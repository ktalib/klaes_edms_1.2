 

## LS Weighing Methods (MSDAM)

### 1. Record to Record Weighting
Assign for the following the value of "**2**":
* **Party 1, 2, 3** = 2
* **Reg Party** = 2
* **Transaction Date** = 2
* **Reg Time** = 2
* **Reg Date** = 2
* **Total = 10**  

---

### 2. Table Weighting (For the MSDAM)
* **FH** = 2.5
* **PRA** = 5
* **DR** = 5
* **COFO** = 5

---

### 3. Timeline Weighing (Only for TL)
* **OP** = 10
* **TOT** = 9
* **ROFO** = 8
* **All other instruments + COFO** =  1
